# Citations and licences

## Assigned course books (not published with this app)

Maxwell, Christian, the Penguin Atlas, Barrows, Tellegen-Couperus, Blaisdell’s *Greek and Roman Oratory*, and the assigned lives remain on the Student Portal. This app does not reproduce those chapters. After a week ends, a review room may discuss them without substituting for the original.

## Primary extracts (public domain)

- Homer, *Odyssey* (opening; sense of the Laertes scene). Greek text in the public domain.
- *Res Gestae Divi Augusti*.
- Dictatus Papae (Gregory VII’s register), in public-domain English wording.
- Short sense of Atrahasis, the type of Ptahhotep, Virgil *Aeneid* 1.33, Tacitus *Agricola* 30 — cited as rooms, not printed at assigned-PDF length.

## Secondary positions (summaries, short fair-use)

Positions are summarized from the scholarly quarrels the rooms name: Wolf, Parry/Lord, Nagy, West (Homer); Gibbon, Pirenne, Brown, Heather, Ward-Perkins (Fall of Rome); Tellenbach, Ullmann, Tierney (Investiture); Polybius as a primary-theoretical voice on the constitution. Quoted blocks are kept short; otherwise the card is a position-summary.
